How to Make a Peanut Butter and Jelly Sandwich

YouTube Tutorial

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of white/wheat/whole grain bread (Your Choice)
  • Peanut Butter
  • Jelly

Process: (FOLLOW DIRECTIONS CAREFULLY!)

  1. Grab a loaf of white/wheat/whole grain bread and take out exactly two slices of bread
  2. Put the two slices of bread into separate slots of the toaster (ensure toaster is on first)
  3. Toast the bread for your desired amount of time
  4. Close the bread bag and store it where you found it
  5. Once bread is finished, grab a clean plate and put the toasted bread on the plate
  6. Open the jars of peanut butter and jelly respectively
  7. Grab a clean butter knife by the handle and first grab a desired amount of peanut butter with the flat end of the knife
  8. Lather the peanut butter evenly on the flat side of one slice of toasted bread
  9. Clean off the flat end of the knife with a clean napkin and carefully grab a desired amount of jelly with that end of the knife
  10. Lather jelly onto the other slice of bread (one without peanut butter)
  11. Place knife off to the side of plate
  12. Close peanut butter and jelly jars
  13. Put the slices of bread on top of each other with the peanut butter and jelly sides facing each other
  14. Enjoy your sandwich
